# Materiality Assessment Worksheet

Step 1 of the ESG framework build. Purpose: produce a signed-off shortlist of the topics
that genuinely matter to the business. Everything downstream (topics, metrics, targets)
filters against this list.

**Our position:** voluntary reporting, investor audience → weight *financial materiality*
high, but still record impact materiality (double materiality) so the output is defensible.

> "Material" simply means "important enough to report on." A topic is material if it could
> reasonably affect the business's value (financial) or if the business affects people and
> the environment through it (impact).

---

## How to use this

1. Fill Part A (context) — facts, one pass.
2. Gather Part B (stakeholder input) — interviews/surveys, no AI.
3. Populate Part C (long list) — start from your sector standard, then add.
4. Score in Part D — humans only. AI may summarise, never score.
5. Apply the threshold in Part E — result is the shortlist.
6. Sign off in Part F — Sustainability + Finance.

**Rule:** this is a business judgement. AI can help organise, but a named human decides.

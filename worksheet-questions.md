# Question Coverage Worksheet

Task: confirm that our assessment questions cover the metrics SASB prescribes for each
disclosure topic. Framework v0.6.0. 28 topics.

## Why this matters

SASB tells us which topics are material for an industry; it also publishes the metrics a
company should disclose under each topic. Our questions are the tool's attempt to assess
those topics. If a SASB-prescribed metric has no question behind it, the tool scores that
topic on incomplete information. This sheet records where that gap is.

## How to fill each entry

- **SASB topic** — the disclosure topic this maps to (from `framework.json` `topicSources`).
- **SASB metrics** — copy the metric(s) SASB prescribes for this topic at industry level.
- **Our questions** — what we currently ask.
- **Coverage verdict** — `covered` (every SASB metric has a question), `partial` (some
  metrics unasked), or `gap` (SASB metric has no question at all).

Where a SASB metric is missing, note whether to add a question or accept the gap, as done
for topics in `worksheet-weightings.md`.
